# Manual visual-assessment guide

Complete these fields only after looking at each photograph. They deliberately remain blank in the automatic inventory because they are subjective scene descriptions, not reliable automatic labels.

| Field | Allowed categories | Use |
|---|---|---|
| View_Type | Close, Medium, Wide | Camera framing of the plants. |
| Vegetation_Density | Low, Medium, High | Amount of visible vegetation. |
| Occlusion | Low, Medium, High | Degree to which relevant plant parts hide one another or are hidden. |
| Trellis_Interference | None, Low, Medium, High | Visual interference from netting, supports, or trellis. |
| Background_Complexity | Low, Medium, High | Non-plant objects, textures, structures, and competing colour. |
| Plant_Visibility | Good, Moderate, Poor | How clearly the target plant material can be seen. |
| Manual_Image_Quality | Good, Moderate, Poor | Overall usefulness after visual inspection, considering blur and exposure. |
| Segmentation_Suitability | Good, Moderate, Poor | Expected feasibility for a first segmentation test, not ground-truth accuracy. |
| Notes | Free text | State the reason for unusual ratings, e.g. person, severe foreground blur, or irrigation equipment. |

Use the same criterion across the dataset. If uncertain, record the uncertainty in `Notes`; do not force a level of precision that the image does not support.
